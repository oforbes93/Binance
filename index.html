Binance Market Share of Total Crypto Market Cap

Overview: This chart tracks Binance’s share of total cryptocurrency market capitalisation over time.

Data sources:
• Binance asset balances: DefiLlama (CEX transparency data)
• Total crypto market cap: TradingView (CRYPTOCAP:TOTAL)

Methodology:
Market share is calculated daily as:
Binance total assets ÷ total crypto market capitalisation

The dataset and chart are automatically refreshed each day via GitHub Actions.
