Binance Market Share of Total Crypto Market Cap

This chart shows Binance’s share of the total cryptocurrency market capitalisation over time.

Binance asset data is sourced from DefiLlama’s centralised exchange transparency dashboards, while total crypto market capitalisation is sourced from TradingView’s CRYPTOCAP:TOTAL series. The series is updated daily via GitHub Actions.

The metric is calculated as Binance total assets divided by total crypto market capitalisation, expressed as a percentage.
