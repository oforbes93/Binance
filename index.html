Binance Exchange Analysis

1. Market Share of Total Crypto Market Cap

2. 24-Hour Net Exchange Flows

Methodology:

  • Market Share: Calculated as Binance's total verified assets (DeFiLlama) divided by the Global Crypto Market Cap (CoinGecko).
  • Net Flows: Represents the net USD value of assets entering or leaving Binance in the last 24 hours. Green indicates net inflows; Red indicates net outflows.

Data is updated daily via automated scripts.

Data Sources: DeFiLlama CEX Transparency & CoinGecko Global API
