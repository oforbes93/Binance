Market Share: Calculated as Binance total assets divided by total crypto market capitalisation.

Net Flow: Represents the net USD value of assets entering or leaving the exchange over a 24-hour period. Green bars indicate net deposits (inflows), while red bars indicate net withdrawals (outflows).

Data sources: DefiLlama (Assets/Flows) and CoinGecko (Global Market Cap). Updated daily.
